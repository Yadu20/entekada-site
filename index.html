EnteKada

WhatsApp chat automation for local businesses in Kerala

EnteKada helps small shops and service businesses answer their customers on WhatsApp, around the clock. Customers message the business's own WhatsApp number as usual; EnteKada replies to common questions and hands anything else to the owner.

We build on the official WhatsApp Business Platform from Meta.
